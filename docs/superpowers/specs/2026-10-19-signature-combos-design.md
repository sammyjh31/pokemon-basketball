# Signature moves and chemistry-led combo plays

## Intent and execution
The user asks for species/type signatures and Pokémon combo plays, with coaches choosing and timing them from mastery and chemistry, and continued GitHub commits/pushes. Extend the shared simulation, without manual coaching chores, loss of history, or a new progress currency. Continue the already selected inline workflow in the existing isolated preview worktree under this explicit implementation/Git authorization; document routine design decisions rather than add permission round trips.

## Rules and catalog
Use independent moveset version 2 (version 1 remains byte-identical, court rules stay 4). New solo/local drafts and next franchise seasons adopt 2. Recorded local transitions reconstruct their recorded version, including 1 and absent. Version 2 requires the existing move prerequisites; combos additionally require chemistry and an enabled ability switch. Old flagless and version-1 reports/results stay unchanged.

Keep the eight existing moves. Add one basketball adaptation per each of 18 types, unlocking at 25 existing mastery XP. Add 14 explicit species/family adaptations at 100 XP: Pikachu family, Charizard, Blastoise, Venusaur, Alakazam, Gengar, Snorlax, Dragonite, Mewtwo, Scizor, Tyranitar, Suicune, Greninja and Lucario. These are game-defined basketball signatures, not claims about canonical battle learnsets. Signatures compete for existing 2/3/4 slots (0/200/400 XP), with existing Power/Control/Efficiency specialization at 75 XP. Coaches value skills, physical tools, tactics, stamina and team needs; upcoming signatures inform automatic practice. No free points, extra random draws or unbounded stacking.

## Combo policy
Four plays: pick-and-roll (screen, received pass, rim attempt), pick-and-pop (screen, received pass, mid/three attempt), drive-and-kick (actual drive, kickout, perimeter attempt), elemental relay (eligible complementary-type pair, pass, shot). Relay links: fire/flying, water/ice, electric/steel, grass/poison, psychic/fighting, ghost/dark, rock/ground, dragon/fairy, normal/bug. Each member needs 25 XP. A deterministic pure policy ranks at most two directed pairs per play from skills, coach/tactic, minimum pair mastery and earned pair familiarity. Cold/new partnerships can run simpler combinations less effectively; retained chemistry improves timing/strength. Active-five eligibility, real roles, fatigue, clocks and player cooldowns control actual execution. Game plan weights favor available pairs without bypassing existing play selection.

## Runtime contract
One combo setup per court segment; both participants must be distinct teammates currently on court, have version-2 move runtimes, sufficient energy, an unused move opportunity and no cooldown. Three-second minimum remaining clock; reserve at least 35 energy before participation. A setup costs each member 3.5 energy scaled by their existing specialization, uses their move opportunity and applies a 100-second elapsed-time cooldown to both. It replaces individual move use for those participants in that segment. Effects are bounded and reset by the existing segment reset. Screen setups affect contact/movement; passing setups affect real interception risk/movement; a shot bonus applies only after the actual successful pass and specified actual shot. A turnover, violation, wrong receiver/zone or changed segment cannot produce a completed combo. Offensive rebounds start fresh segments; cooldowns survive bench time.

Combo strength is bounded by pair mastery/familiarity and coach risk/fit. No new win/score shortcuts. Existing simulation handles the final shot, foul, rebound and box score. Coaches select plays and receiver options automatically from the same playbook; shared roster evaluation/training consumes bounded pure projections without recursion into coachPolicy or teamEvaluation.

## Persistence and reports
Optional combo attempts/shots-created counters live inside move stats and merge/validate exactly through season, playoff and career totals. Each participant gets one participation counter, not two invented team possessions. Actual setup/shot events carry both player identities, elapsed time and costs; completing means a shot was created, not necessarily made. Frozen pregame pair assignments derive on detailed replay like move assignments, not stored for every fixture. Strict parsing rejects unknown combos, invalid/negative/fractional counts and completions exceeding attempts. Mixed old/new career history must reconcile exact move/combo totals.

Current mastery panels show equipped signature eligibility, unlock XP and coach pair plans. Game reports show frozen pair assignments and real recorded attempts/shots-created/energy. Keep old reports absent rather than invent data. All simulated regular seasons, playoffs, CPU/local fixtures and historical boxes use the shared runtime; officially played arcade boxes retain their existing rejection of simulated move telemetry. Mastery/chemistry continue through existing season, trade, draft and free-agent flows.
