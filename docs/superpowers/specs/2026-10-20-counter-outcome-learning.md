# Counter outcome learning

Extend existing coach scouting so automatic plans respond to actual opponent-specific counter results, across every shared simulated game and franchise boundaries. New moveset rules v4 preserve omitted/v1/v2/v3 games exactly. Existing seasons adopt v4 at the next franchise boundary; new solo/local drafts use v4.

Each observed attack records a response once, whether used or withheld: points, scoreless attacks, turnovers/violations, fouls, paid energy and offensive counter-answers. Defensive results favor fewer points allowed; offensive counter-combos favor more points scored. Compare with observed non-use attacks using neutral priors, effective sample confidence and a bounded energy cost. Small samples remain exploratory. Descriptive comparisons do not establish causality: availability, lineups and matchups differ.

Coaches adjust deterministic response frequency/cooldowns and offensive combo play weights. No extra random draws, free shooting bonus or guaranteed stops. Even preferred counters are occasionally withheld, and poor counters are occasionally tested. Eligibility, mastery, active-player checks, chemistry and energy constraints remain authoritative. Learn only after the attack's shooting/free throws complete; freeze pregame plans separately from current-game evidence. Compact season simulations collect the same facts as detailed games.
