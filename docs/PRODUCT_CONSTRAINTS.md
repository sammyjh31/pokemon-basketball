# Product constraints

Confirmed by the user on 2026-10-16:

- Do not introduce evolution-based development. A player keeps its species.
- Do not introduce injuries, injury recovery, aging, retirement or related career-lifecycle systems. Existing in-game fatigue and offseason skill development remain allowed.
- Do not introduce trading of draft picks. Existing trades of players remain allowed.
- New league signature abilities are enabled by default and must have small, balanced effects.
- Support two local franchise modes: start with Generation I and introduce the next generation in each two-round offseason rookie draft; or start with all bundled generations and have no rookie drafts in future seasons. Both modes retain player development.
