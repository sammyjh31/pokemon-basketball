# Coach scouting continuity

Carry coach-specific scouting and counter knowledge across solo and local franchise seasons. Knowledge follows trainer identity when a coach changes jobs; a new coach also receives weaker club handover reports. Known shooting tendencies follow individual Pokémon after trades. Team move patterns lose confidence with roster turnover and opposing coach changes; pair-combo knowledge scales by retained pair opportunities. Rookies have no invented personal evidence. Knowledge ages, with a bounded eight-season lookback.

Store versioned compact annual coach books (coaches, team roster/coach metadata, observed opponent scouting) as optional historical summaries. Capture final regular/postseason evidence without replaying the whole league. At a new season, derive optional versioned per-team scouting seeds from those books and actual post-offseason appointments/rosters. Merge duplicate personal/club observations conservatively (choose stronger evidence, never add overlapping reports). Round carried observations to three decimals. Old seasons without seeds replay identically; old summaries without books remain valid. Existing v3 counter arithmetic is unchanged; only explicit new season inputs change.

Season builders, standalone simulated matches, partial advances, CPU games, playoffs and archive boxes use the seeded existing scouting path. Optional carried metadata allows known individual evidence even when a new club has never faced that opponent; never fabricate meetings. Validate books against archived rosters/coaches and replayed completed observations, validate seeds against preceding books at boundaries, include all data in authenticated existing replay evidence and preserve warm restoration. No authoritative mutable root bank, new dependencies or changes to arcade physics.

Coach and box reports explain carried evidence, staff handovers, retained rosters and changed opposing coaches. Coaches manage this automatically. No user saves are inspected or changed during verification. Commit, push, merge and publish are already authorized.
