# Make the current decision clear

The user reported information overload across the whole game. Keep the established retro Pokémon / basketball styling and animations, but lead with the task and reveal detailed information on request. This is a first pass through the main draft, team, season and postseason flows; it does not claim an exhaustive redesign of every franchise or arcade subpanel.

## Observed problems

1. On the current hosted desktop draft, the large empty court and setup/sharing controls pushed player choices below the fold. An older already-open session also showed the cost of stacked empty bench / zero-minute reports. Captures: `03-latest-draft.png` is the current-release baseline; `01-before-draft.png` and `02-before-pick.png` are explicitly older-session evidence, not the current-release baseline.
2. Local drafting placed the confirmation action after the entire lineup and team-rating table. Ranking chips, glossary and rules competed with finding a player.
3. The saved local postseason showed a repeated stage tracker and full bracket, with the current round far down the page. Detailed league tables continued underneath the main results.

## Implemented behavior

- Solo player choices come before the court in both DOM and visual order. Desktop challenge offers share one four-card row; phones retain two columns. Court and bench remain accessible via Team navigation below the board. Franchise roster layout retains its existing structure.
- Draft rules group generation, legendary and shiny settings, keeping the current pool and legend status visible in the summary. Changing these settings retains existing confirmation and disabled-state safeguards.
- Sharing, sprite style and records live under Share & customize (initially open in lineup-builder mode, where Randomize and Clear are useful primary tools). Detailed selected-player scouting is optional; the existing dock remains the primary position/pick action.
- Shared scouting keeps identity, role, fit and summary visible; abilities, personality and detailed tools remain in Full scouting report. Bench rotation and team strengths, plus trainer/development reports, are expandable. Legacy saves without these systems do not show an empty disclosure; missing portrait fallbacks are still treated as filled players.
- Onboarding names one Up next action. The full checklist remains under All steps.
- Local drafting keeps search, basic sorting, recommendations and comparisons available. Role-specific ranking shortcuts, glossary and rule explanations move into the existing Advanced scouting section. Team impact leads with its biggest lift and tradeoff, with the complete before/after table available on request. Confirmation comes before the long lineup report and explicitly warns that suggested positions are included. The inspector identity is compact and remains above the roster stacking layer.
- Completed solo seasons lead with their recap; detailed player/league reports and the 82-game schedule open separately. Local results keep friends' visual summaries, with duplicate tabular rankings and whole-league reports expandable.
- Playoffs default to current-round matchups on every screen size, retaining future-round browsing and the full bracket. The duplicated stage tracker is omitted in round view. Compact framing and friend status cards bring the live round and matchups closer to simulation controls.
- Removed the duplicate Season header link; the existing section navigation remains available.

No engine rules, rating calculations, draft probabilities, saved formats or simulation guards changed. All primary controls use their existing handlers. Disclosures are native details/summary controls with keyboard focus styling, using the existing retro font and colors.
