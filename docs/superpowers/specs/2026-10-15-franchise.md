# Franchise mode

Continue a completed model-3 league after its Finals. The existing season becomes franchise Year 1; entering the offseason archives it exactly once. All 30 eight-player rosters carry forward, maintaining 240 unique Gen I–II species. Offseason one-for-one trades exchange existing roster slots. Other clubs evaluate ability and fit, with a clear offer preview and rejection reason. No redrafts, free agents, contracts, aging or CPU-initiated trades in this increment.

Starting the next season increments the year, uses a fresh supplied seed, resets only current-season results/playoffs and keeps the league rosters. The user can reorder their eight players and change coaching before simulation. Franchise seasons do not inflate the original challenge records. Each new season has 82 games and the existing 16-team best-of-seven playoffs. The active season retains full player stats and replayable box scores.

Career identity is Pokémon species ID, independent of team. Track regular-season and playoff counting totals separately, games, starts, shooting percentages, highs, double/triple doubles and player-of-game awards. Completed-season history records all team records/rosters, league awards, All-League/All-Defense selections, champion, Finals result and Finals MVP; these establish each player's honors and championship rings even after trades. Career totals include archived seasons and can show live current-season additions without storing them twice.

Persistence stays backward compatible with the v3 save key and adds a versioned franchise capsule plus explicit fixed-roster season model 4. Original model-3 replay is unchanged. Revalidate the active season deterministically; archived career totals/history use bounded structural and cross-reference validation, not replay of every past game. Keep only the active season's game-level data, so save size and load work do not grow by an entire league replay each year. Explain this limit in the history UI. Save errors remain visible. Starting a fresh draft explicitly warns that it ends the saved franchise.
